Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any setup fees or monthly retainers?

No. OTP88 operates on a 100% transparent pay-as-you-go model. You never pay for account creation, API keys, or monthly platform access.

Do you charge for undelivered OTP messages?

No. If a message fails delivery due to carrier network unavailability or invalid recipient, OTP88 does not charge you.

Which payment currencies and methods are supported?

We support credit/debit cards, bank wires, DuitNow / FPX (Malaysia), PayNow (Singapore), Stripe, and corporate invoicing in USD, MYR, and SGD.
